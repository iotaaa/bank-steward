# Bank Steward

Sorts your bank into categories, shows you the finished layout instantly, and guides you through tidying it one move at a time.

Pick which tab each category goes in, press **Preview** to see the result, then press **Organise** and follow the highlights. Bank Steward works out the fewest moves needed and keeps up if you do things your own way.

- See your whole bank organised before you move anything
- Plans every tab, not just the one you're looking at
- Keeps related items together, from armour sets to herbs and their secondaries

## Features

- **Preview:** shows your whole bank already organised, each category on its own rows, as a tag tab you can withdraw from straight away.
- **Organise:** highlights the item to move and where it goes, with an arrow between them. Items go into the right tab first, then each tab is put in order.
- **Keeps up with you:** if you move something differently, it works out the next best move. If the spot is scrolled out of view, it points at the edge of the bank so you can drag and scroll.
- **Tab icons:** each tab's first item is its icon, so it stays where it is, marked in gold. You can turn this off.
- **Insert mode:** moves inside a tab need the bank's Insert mode, and the guide reminds you if it's off.
- **Categories:** 11 to start with, from Money & clues to High alch. Rename, recolour, reorder, delete or add your own, and pick a tab for each.
- **Right-click filing:** change an item's category, choose **Leave where it is**, or file every item in a tab at once. Name patterns like `*bones, dragon *` catch whole groups.
- **Smart ordering:** gear by combat style, then weapon kind and armour set. Skilling items and resources go skill by skill, potions highest dose first, and food by how much it heals.
- **Items that belong together:** items sharing a word sit together (all your buckets, all your burnt food), and items used with each other sit side by side, like each unfinished potion and its secondary.
- **Bank markers:** optional category colours on items and under tab headers, with adjustable opacity, plus a mark on items in the wrong tab.
- **Alch Ledger:** with [Alch Ledger](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/alch-ledger) on, its outlines hide while you organise, and profitable alchs can go in the High alch category.
