# Bank Steward

Sorts your bank into categories, shows you the finished layout instantly, and guides you through tidying it one move at a time.

## Features

**Preview.** See your whole bank already organised, with each category on its own rows. It opens as a tag tab, so you can withdraw from it straight away without moving anything.

**Organise.** Follow the highlights to move one item at a time, with an arrow from the item to where it goes.
- Plans the whole bank: items go into the right tab first, then each tab is put in order, using the fewest moves possible.
- If you do something different, it just works out the next move.
- If the spot is scrolled out of view, it points at the edge of the bank so you can drag and scroll.
- Each tab's first item is its icon, so it stays where it is and is marked in gold.
- Turn on **Insert** mode at the bottom of the bank first. The guide reminds you if it's off.

**Categories.** 11 to start with: Money & clues, Gear, Teleports, Runes & ammo, Potions, Food, Skilling, Resources, Cosmetic, Quest & misc and High alch.
- Rename, recolour, reorder, delete or add your own, and pick a bank tab for each.
- Right-click a bank item to change its category, or a bank tab to file everything in it.
- Add name patterns like `*bones, dragon *` to catch whole groups of items.

**Smart ordering.**
- Gear by combat style, then weapon kind and armour set, strongest first.
- Skilling items and resources skill by skill: tools, outfit, bags, then materials by tier.
- Potions in a sensible order, highest dose first. Food by how much it heals.
- Items in your bank that share a word sit together, like all your buckets or burnt food.
- Items used together sit side by side in the same tab, like each unfinished potion and its secondary.

**Alch Ledger.** If you also use [Alch Ledger](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/alch-ledger), its bank outlines hide while you organise, and you can put profitable items in the High alch category.
