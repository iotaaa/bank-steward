# Bank Steward

Sorts your bank into categories, shows you the finished layout instantly, and guides you through tidying it one move at a time.

Pick which tab each category goes in, press **Preview** to see the result, then press **Organise** and follow the highlights. Bank Steward works out the fewest moves needed and keeps up if you do things your own way.

- See your whole bank organised before you move anything
- Plans every tab, not just the one you're looking at
- Keeps related items together, from armour sets to herbs and their secondaries

## Features

### Preview

- Shows your whole bank already organised, with each category on its own rows.
- Opens as a tag tab, so you can withdraw from it straight away.

### Organise

- Highlights the item to move and where it goes, with an arrow between them.
- Moves items into the right tab first, then puts each tab in order.
- Keeps each tab's first item as its icon. Turn on **Insert** mode before you start.

### Categories

- 11 to start with, from Money & clues to High alch.
- Rename, recolour, reorder or add your own, and pick a tab for each.
- Right-click an item or a tab to change categories.

### Smart ordering

- Gear by set, skilling items by skill, potions by dose, food by healing.
- Items that share a word sit together, like all your buckets.
- Items used together sit side by side, like each unfinished potion and its secondary.

### Alch Ledger

- With [Alch Ledger](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/alch-ledger) on, its outlines hide while you organise.
- Profitable alchs can go in the High alch category.
